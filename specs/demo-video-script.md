# Demo Video Script — Maloba (2–3 min)

> Recording-ready shot list for the concept submission video. **Target: web build** (`cd app &&
> npm run web`) — easiest to screen-record cleanly; Expo Go on a phone is the fallback if you'd rather
> show it on a real device. Total runtime ~2:50. Record in one take per shot; narrate live or add
> voice-over after. Keep Wi-Fi ON except for the offline beat (shot 4).
>
> **Before you hit record:** run `npm run gen:images` once (art is cached), have the app open on the
> Home gallery, and do one dry run of the record → consent → delete flow so it's smooth on camera.

---

## Shot 1 — Hook (0:00–0:20)

**On screen:** Home gallery. Four literary pillars + the Cultural Atlas section. Scroll slowly once.
**Say:** *"This is Maloba — Setswana for 'yesterday'. Mantswe a maloba: the voices of yesterday. It
brings South Africa's foundational literature and heritage to life — offline, multilingual, and free."*

## Shot 2 — Humanities depth (0:20–1:00)

**Do:** Tap **Ityala Lamawele** → open the *inkundla* (the lawsuit) scene. Let the cinematic image land.
**Say:** *"Mqhayi's 1914 novel — the case of the twins Wele and Babini, argued in the inkundla. This is
Xhosa customary law and restorative justice: ubulungisa, umthetho — decided by the community, not
imposed on it."*
**Do:** Open **About the Sources**. Point to the real citations.
**Say:** *"Every word is grounded. Plaatje, Mqhayi, Mutwa, Vilakazi — real texts, real references. The
technology serves the humanities, never the other way around."*

## Shot 3 — Breadth: the Cultural Atlas (1:00–1:25)

**Do:** Back to Home → open the **Cultural Atlas** → **Unsung Heroes** → Kgosi Galeshewe (or Marriage
Rites → *lobola*).
**Say:** *"Around the canon we've built a cited Cultural Atlas — the heroes history nearly forgot, the
customs, the peopling of the land. And every entry asks: what's your family's version? — pulling
communities straight into the archive."*

## Shot 4 — Accessibility (1:25–2:00)

**Do:** On any open scene: toggle **Child Mode**, then switch language to **Setswana**.
**Say:** *"Two reading levels — the tone changes, the facts never do. And it reads in Setswana, with a
framework built for eleven of the twelve official languages."*
**Do:** Tap **Listen / Reetsa** — let it speak a line. Then **turn off Wi-Fi** and scroll/read.
**Say:** *"It reads aloud — built for African-made neural voices from Botlhale AI. And with the Wi-Fi
off — it still works. This is for real phones, on real networks."*

## Shot 5 — Community + ethics (2:00–2:40)

**Do:** Open **Community Archive** → tap **Record** → the **POPIA consent sheet** appears. Pause on it.
**Say:** *"This is where communities become archivists of their own histories. Before anything is
recorded, a clear POPIA consent step — a person's voice is personal information."*
**Do:** Consent → record a 3-second clip → show it in the list → play it → **delete** it.
**Say:** *"Record, keep it private or share it, and delete it at any time. Consent and the right to
erasure are built into the architecture, not bolted on."*

## Shot 6 — Provenance + close (2:40–2:55)

**Do:** Open the **Heritage Ledger** → tap a **"Verify on Solana"** link (or just show it).
**Say:** *"Our adaptation of each work is notarised on-chain — hashes and citations only, never
anyone's personal data — so what the app says about a text can't be quietly changed. Three of the
four are public domain; the fourth is in copyright and we summarise it in our own words."*
**Close on the Home gallery. Say:** *"Zero cost. Community-owned. On African terms — and built with
African AI. That's Maloba."*

## Recording checklist

- [ ] `npm run gen:images` run (all 7 heroes cached) so no image pops in live on camera.
- [ ] Setswana text + the **Reetsa** label reviewed (so nothing on screen is wrong-in-language).
- [ ] Consent → record → play → delete rehearsed once.
- [ ] Screen recorder at 1080p; hide desktop clutter / browser bookmarks bar.
- [ ] One clean take of the offline beat (toggle Wi-Fi visibly).
- [ ] Keep it under 3:00 — the shot timings above total ~2:55.
